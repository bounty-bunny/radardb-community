# INC-2017: Payment authorization failures

Severity: Sev1
Date: 2026-08-29
Duration: 34 minutes
Incident commander: Marcus Webb
Services: payments-api

## Summary
Stripe returned HTTP 502 errors for card authorizations in eu-west. Payment authorization errors peaked at 38% and roughly 2,900 checkouts failed.

## Root cause
The outage was external (Stripe). Our impact was extended because failing over to Adyen required manual approval from the payments incident commander, who was not reachable for 18 minutes.

## Resolution
payments.provider.fallback was enabled at 09:52 UTC and errors returned to normal within 3 minutes.

## Follow-ups
- PAY-341: automate provider failover when error thresholds are met (owner: Marcus Webb)
- PAY-342: add a secondary approver to the payments IC rotation
