# INC-2041: Checkout latency regression

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-09-14
Duration: 47 minutes (14:55 to 15:42 UTC)
Incident commander: Lena Chen
Services: checkout-svc, pricing-svc, orders-db

## Summary
After DEP-1187 released checkout-svc v4.12.0, checkout p99 latency rose from 620 ms to 2.4 s and CheckoutLatencyHigh fired. About 6% of checkouts timed out during the window.

## Root cause
Commit a91f3c2 (PR #3318, stacked promotions in the cart summary) loaded promotion rules once per cart line instead of once per cart. A typical cart issued 41 queries per checkout instead of 3. The extra queries saturated the PgBouncer pool for checkout (97% utilization), so order writes queued behind promotion reads.

## Detection
CheckoutLatencyHigh paged the Team Falcon on-call 12 minutes after the release. There was no alert on pool saturation at the time.

## Resolution
The on-call rolled back checkout-svc to v4.11.3 (DEP-1188). Latency recovered at 15:42 UTC. The fix, batching the promotion lookup, shipped two days later in PR #3342.
