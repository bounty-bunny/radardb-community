# INC-2055: Delayed order confirmation emails

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-09-22
Services: notification-worker

## Summary
Order confirmation emails were delayed by up to 3 hours. NotificationConsumerLag fired when lag on the orders topic reached 1.2 million messages.

## Root cause
DEP-1192 rolled out version 7 of the order.created schema. notification-worker could not deserialize the new field gift_options and retried the same poison message forever, blocking its partition.

## Resolution
Team Hermes deployed notification-worker 3.4.1 with a dead-letter queue; poison messages now move to orders.dlq after 3 attempts.

- NOT-58: add schema compatibility check to the orders topic CI (owner: Aisha Bello)
