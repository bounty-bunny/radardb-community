# INC-1988: Inventory oversell

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-08-03
Services: inventory-svc

## Summary
During the Back-to-School flash sale, 214 orders were accepted for items that were out of stock.

## Root cause
inventory-svc protected reservations with a Redis lock whose TTL of 2 seconds was shorter than the slowest reservation transaction. When the lock expired, two requests could reserve the same unit.

## Resolution
PR #3190 replaced the Redis lock with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED on the stock row. Customer support refunded the affected orders.
