# INC-1865: Fraud vendor rate limiting

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-04-20
Duration: 130 minutes
Incident commander: Elena Rossi
Services: fraud-svc, payments-api

## Summary
12% of payments were sent to manual review for an hour.

## Root cause
fraud-svc exceeded the vendor's 300 requests per second limit after retries were added without backoff.

## Resolution
Added token-bucket client side rate limiting and exponential backoff.
