# INC-1956: Adyen webhook signature failures

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-07-27
Duration: 19 minutes
Incident commander: Tom Okafor
Services: payments-api

## Summary
Refund confirmations were not recorded for 3 hours.

## Root cause
The HMAC key for Adyen webhooks was rotated in the Adyen console but not in our secrets manager.

## Resolution
Updated the key and documented webhook key rotation in the payments runbook.
