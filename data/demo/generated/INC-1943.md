# INC-1943: Search index rebuild overloaded catalog-svc

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-06-01
Duration: 23 minutes
Incident commander: Hana Sato
Services: search-svc, catalog-svc

## Summary
Product pages were slow for 50 minutes while search reindexed.

## Root cause
The nightly reindex ran in the daytime after a cron timezone change and read catalog-svc without throttling.

## Resolution
Pinned the cron timezone to UTC and throttled reindex reads.
