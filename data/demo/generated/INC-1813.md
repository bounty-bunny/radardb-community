# INC-1813: Expired TLS certificate on shipping-svc

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-03-21
Duration: 144 minutes
Incident commander: Mei Tanaka
Services: shipping-svc

## Summary
Shipping quotes failed for 41 minutes, so checkout fell back to flat-rate shipping.

## Root cause
cert-manager could not renew the certificate because the ACME account secret had been rotated by hand.

## Resolution
Restored the secret and added an alert on certificates expiring within 14 days.
