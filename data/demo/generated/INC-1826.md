# INC-1826: Kafka rebalance storm on orders topic

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-03-22
Duration: 111 minutes
Incident commander: Nina Petrova
Services: notification-worker, fraud-svc

## Summary
Consumers on the orders topic rebalanced continuously for 35 minutes and processing stalled.

## Root cause
A rolling restart with max.poll.interval.ms below the slowest batch made consumers leave the group repeatedly.

## Resolution
Raised max.poll.interval.ms to 10 minutes and switched to cooperative sticky assignment.
