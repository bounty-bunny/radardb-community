# INC-1839: redis-sessions memory eviction

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-04-23
Duration: 47 minutes
Incident commander: Elena Rossi
Services: edge-gateway, redis-sessions

## Summary
Shoppers were logged out mid-checkout; roughly 3% of sessions were lost.

## Root cause
maxmemory-policy was allkeys-lru and a marketing batch job wrote large keys into the session cluster.

## Resolution
Moved the batch job to its own Redis and set maxmemory-policy to volatile-lru.
