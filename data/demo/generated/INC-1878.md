# INC-1878: Tax rounding errors in EU orders

Severity: Sev3
Date: 2026-05-16
Duration: 161 minutes
Incident commander: Raj Patel
Services: tax-svc

## Summary
About 1,100 EU orders were charged one cent more VAT than displayed.

## Root cause
tax-svc rounded per line item while the cart displayed totals rounded per order.

## Resolution
Unified rounding to per-order banker's rounding and refunded affected customers.

- PAY-520: prevent recurrence (owner: Jonas Weber)
- PLAT-752: add a regression test or alert (owner: Raj Patel)
