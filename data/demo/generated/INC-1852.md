# INC-1852: Pricing cache stampede after deploy

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-04-11
Duration: 133 minutes
Incident commander: Nina Petrova
Services: pricing-svc, checkout-svc

## Summary
pricing-svc CPU hit 100% for 9 minutes after a deploy flushed the promotion cache.

## Root cause
All pods rebuilt the promotion rule cache at the same moment because cache keys had no jittered expiry.

## Resolution
Added request coalescing and jittered TTLs to the promotion cache.
