# INC-1891: DNS resolution failures in eu-west

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-05-01
Duration: 109 minutes
Incident commander: Aisha Bello
Services: edge-gateway, payments-api

## Summary
Intermittent 503 responses for 18 minutes in eu-west.

## Root cause
CoreDNS pods were throttled by a too-low CPU limit after a node pool migration.

## Resolution
Raised CoreDNS limits and enabled NodeLocal DNSCache.
