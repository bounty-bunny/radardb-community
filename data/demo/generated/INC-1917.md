# INC-1917: Memory leak in edge-gateway

Severity: Sev2
Date: 2026-06-17
Duration: 59 minutes
Incident commander: Kwame Mensah
Services: edge-gateway

## Summary
edge-gateway pods were OOM-killed every 3 hours, causing short bursts of 502s.

## Root cause
HTTP/3 connection state was not released when mobile clients migrated networks.

## Resolution
Upgraded the QUIC library and capped idle connection lifetime at 5 minutes.
