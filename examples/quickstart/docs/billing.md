# Billing and invoices

## Billing cycle
Quillstack bills workspaces monthly on the day the subscription started. Annual plans are invoiced once per year and receive two months free.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep paid features until then.

## Failed payments
If a card payment fails, Quillstack retries the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days. After the third failed attempt the workspace becomes read-only until an owner updates the payment method.
